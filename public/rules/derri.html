How to play Derri në dorë

Derri në dorë (“pig in hand”) is played by four. You make pairs and throw them away until you run out of cards. Whoever is left holding the K♠, the pig, loses.

4 players · 49 cards

Play Derri në dorë

The cards

  • The other three kings are removed, leaving 49 cards. The K♠, the pig, pairs with nothing.
  • One player gets 13 cards, the others 12.
  • Everyone immediately throws away the pairs they hold: two cards of the same number or face.
7♥7♣Q♦Q♠K♠

Two pairs are thrown. The K♠ always stays in the hand.

How to play

  • On your turn, take one card without looking from the player on your left. The player on your right takes from you.
  • If the card you took pairs with one of yours, both are thrown. Otherwise it stays in your hand.
  • Whoever runs out of cards is out and safe. After that, cards are taken from the next player who still has some.
  • In the end only one player is left, holding the pig. They lose the hand.

Derri në dorë online on VITOWEB

  • Press LUAJ (Play) for a table with players online, or create a room for your friends.
  • Tap a card to lift it, tap it again to take it. You have 20 seconds.
  • PRIT (Wait) gives you 3 more seconds to rearrange your cards before the other player picks from you, once per turn. While they pick, your hand stays still.
