How to play Imposter

Imposter is a party game for 3 to 12 people. Everyone gets the same secret word, except the imposter. They have to act as if they know it, and the others have to find them.

3–12 people · one phone or each with their own

Play Imposter

Setting up

  • One phone: the phone goes from hand to hand and each person sees their own card.
  • Each with their own phone: one person creates the room and shares the code or the link. Every phone shows only its own card, and the host runs the round.
  • Choose how long the talking lasts, or play without a timer.

How to play

  • Everyone gets the same word, except the imposter.
  • In turn, each player says one word connected to the secret word. Not too obvious, so the imposter can't work it out, and not too far off, so nobody suspects you.
  • When you think you know who it is, vote out loud. If you vote wrong, the game goes on with the same imposter.
  • When time is up, the phone rings. Take one more minute, or the host reveals the imposter.
  • With more players you can have two imposters. With the 1 or 2 option, nobody knows whether there are one or two.
