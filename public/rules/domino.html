How to play Domino

Domino is played with 28 tiles, from 0|0 to 6|6. Tiles are placed at either end of the line and the numbers must match. Whoever runs out of tiles first scores the points left in the opponents' hands.

2 or 4 players · 28 tiles · to 100 points

Play Domino

Tiles and players

  • 28 tiles, from 0|0 to 6|6. Each player gets 7.
  • 2 vs 2: you and your partner opposite, against the two players at the sides. All 28 tiles are dealt.
  • 1 vs 1: the 14 spare tiles are either drawn when you have no tile that fits, or stay out of play. You choose before the game.

How to play

  • The first hand is opened by whoever holds the highest double (in 2 vs 2, the 6|6), and they open with it.
  • From the second hand on, the player who went out first in the last hand opens.
  • A tile goes at either end of the line. Its number must match that end.
  • If you have no tile that fits, you pass. In 1 vs 1 with drawing, you draw until you find a tile that fits.

Scoring

  • When someone runs out of tiles, their side scores the points of the tiles left in the opponents' hands. A tile is worth the sum of its two halves: 6|6 is worth 12.
  • The 0|0 is worth 10 points when it is the only tile left in a hand. Together with other tiles it is worth 0.
  • If the game is blocked and nobody can move, the side with fewer points in hand wins and scores the opponents' points. On a tie, nobody scores.
  • The first side to reach 100 points wins. On VITOWEB you can also choose 50, 150 or another target.
