How to play Mafia

Mafia is played as a group, around a table. Each player gets a secret role on their own phone, and one of you, the narrator, runs the game. The village has to find the mafia before the mafia matches the village in number.

The roles

RoleSideWhat they do
MafiaMafiaAt night they open their eyes together with the other mafia members and choose whom to kill. By day they must not be found out.
DoctorVillageEvery night chooses someone to save. They can save themselves too.
Police officerVillageEvery night picks someone they suspect. The narrator tells them whether that person is mafia. They don't say they are the police officer.
VillagerVillageNo night power. Finds the mafia by voting during the day.
NarratorNeitherGets no role. Sees everyone's role and runs the night and the day.

Setting up

  • One person creates the room and shares the code or the link. The others join on their own phones.
  • The host chooses how many mafia, doctors and police officers there will be. Everyone else is a villager. The mafia must be fewer than half.
  • The host hands out the roles. Each player sees their role by holding the screen down; when they let go, the role is hidden.

The night

  • The narrator says: “The village sleeps.” Everyone closes their eyes.
  • The mafia open their eyes and, without talking, choose whom to kill.
  • The doctor opens their eyes and chooses whom to save.
  • The police officer opens their eyes and points at someone they suspect. The narrator signals yes or no.

The day

  • The narrator says who was killed in the night, or that the doctor saved them.
  • Whoever was killed is out of the game and doesn't speak any more.
  • The village discusses and votes on whom to throw out. Whoever gets the most votes is out.

Winning

  • The village wins when all the mafia are out.
  • The mafia win when they are as many as everyone else.
  • At the end, the narrator can reveal all the roles on the phones.
