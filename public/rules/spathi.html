How to play Spathi

Spathi is an Albanian card game for two. You play one card at a time and capture the cards on the table that have the same value or add up to it. Points come from the number of cards, clubs, the 2♣ and the 10♦.

2 players · 52 cards · to 21 points

Play Spathi

Cards and the deal

  • Played with 52 cards, no jokers, by two players.
  • Each player gets 4 cards and 4 cards are laid face up on the table.
  • When both hands are empty, 4 more are dealt to each, until the deck runs out.

Values

  • The ace is worth 1. From 2 to 10, a card is worth its number.
  • J, Q and K don't add up: each one captures only the same face card.

Capturing

  • On your turn you play one card. It captures the table cards of the same value, or groups of cards that add up to its value, up to 10.
  • You can capture several groups at once: a 10 takes 4+6 and 3+7.
  • If your card can capture, it must. If there are several ways, you choose.
  • If it captures nothing, the card stays on the table.
  • At the end of the hand, the cards left on the table go to whoever captured last.

Points each hand

Each hand gives out 5 points.

Winning

  • The first to reach 21 points wins, or 11 in the short game.
  • If both pass the target in the same hand, the higher score wins. On a tie, one more hand is played.
